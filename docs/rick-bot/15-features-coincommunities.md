# Feature — CoinCommunities (`/com` / `/comms`)

> **Resumen (ES):** Obtén una vista rápida de la actividad reciente, top KOLs y top posts de la comunidad de cualquier token. Disponible en Telegram y Discord. Fuente: [talk.markets/t/coincommunities-are-live-on-rick/8542](https://talk.markets/t/coincommunities-are-live-on-rick/8542).

---

## What are CoinCommunities?

CoinCommunities gives you an **instant overview** of any token's community, including:

- Recent activity
- Top KOLs
- Top posts

Community links are also added to the `/soc` command (or `.soc` on Discord).

---

## Commands

| Platform | Command | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram | `/com` / `/comms` | Get community overview for a token |
| Discord | `.com` / `.soc` | Discord equivalents |
