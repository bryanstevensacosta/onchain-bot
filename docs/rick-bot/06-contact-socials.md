## How to give feedback or report a bug

### Quickest path: in-Telegram `/burpback`

Use `/burpback <feedback>` directly inside Telegram. You can also **reply to a message** to attach that message's context to your report.

### Discord (preferred for support tickets)

Open a ticket in the official Discord support server: **[Rick's Lab](https://talk.markets/dc)**.
