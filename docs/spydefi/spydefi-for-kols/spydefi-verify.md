# SpyDefi Verify

We are aware of not only how rife impersonators are in the space, but also of the current inability for KOLs to 'verify' themselves to clients, and others, through a trusted third party.

#### ✅ SpyDefi Verify Core Features

➡️ **One-step verification** - With one simple action, users can check any user @ or channel @ through the bot and be notified if they are verified and legitimate

➡️ **Two-way verification protocol** - Checking a user's @ will not only confirm they are legitimate but also note all the channels they own. Vice versa, checking a channel's @ will provide all the associated, verified owners of the channel.&#x20;

➡️ **Scammer warning system** - Verified KOLs can notify the SpyDefi team of any impersonating user @s or channels @s they have, a scam warning tag will then be applied to that @ if anyone tries to verify its legitimacy with us.&#x20;

➡️ **Heimdall protection** - [t.me/heimdall\_odintools\_bot](https://t.me/heimdall_odintools_bot), the market leader for anti-scam and anti-spam in Telegram groups, will also globally blacklist any impersonators to mitigate the risk of them successfully scamming any projects.&#x20;
