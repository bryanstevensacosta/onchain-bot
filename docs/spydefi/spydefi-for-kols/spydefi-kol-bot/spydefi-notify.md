# SpyDefi Notify

Simply head to [t.me/spydefikolbot](https://t.me/spydefikolbot) and type /follow to set up this service.

{% hint style="success" %}
Anytime any one of your calls hits an X, you will be sent the relevant achievement post straight to your DMs inside the bot.
{% endhint %}

Benefits

1. No need for KOLs to try track their own Xs on calls manually, which can be a burden for many, as the bot will do it all for you&#x20;
2. Minimised risk of older called projects that randomly have gone up being missed as an oversight to update by KOLs
3. KOLs don't have to search the t.me/spydefi feed for recent achievement posts and also they don't have to keep checking charts of their calls for ATHs/Xs, as the bot will notify them.

{% hint style="info" %}
This is a huge QoL tool and especially good for KOLs who make a higher number of posts.
{% endhint %}
