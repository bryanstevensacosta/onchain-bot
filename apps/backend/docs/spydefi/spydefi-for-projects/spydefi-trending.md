# SpyDefi Trending

SpyDefi Trending is interwoven into the SpyDefi ecosystem, seeing trending projects all over.&#x20;

With space wide adoption, SpyDefi through its trending, provides projects the chance for projects to gain huge exposure, through various potential avenues.&#x20;

#### 🏆 SpyDefi Leaderboard&#x20;

Trending projects in the SpyDefi trending leaderboard, that is pinned in the SpyDefi feed [@spydefi ](https://t.me/spydefi).

#### 🔄 Listing Alert

Trending projects will receive a listing alert, that is printed in the SpyDefi feed and directly messaged to the individual who booked, allowing them to share with their community.&#x20;

#### 💬 Achievement Posts

Trending projects will appear at the bottomf of some achievement posts, that are regularly shared by KOLs in their own channels.&#x20;

#### 🤖 User Scans

Trending projects will appear at the bottom of some user scans on the SpyDefi main bot&#x20;

( [t.me/spydefi\_bot](https://t.me/spydefi_bot) )

## The Data

💬 Thousands of daily users of the[ t.me/Spydefi](https://t.me/Spydefi) feed

📞 1500+ daily shares of [@SpyDefi](https://t.me/Spydefi) content by KOLs&#x20;

👤 10,000+ daily scans by users of the [@Spydefi\_bot](https://t.me/spydefi_bot) with 140,000 lifetime users

⚡️Providing an average of 600,000 daily culminative views&#x20;

## *Trending Description Feature*

Projects can add a 35 character description when trending on SpyDefi, to provide key context.&#x20;

➡️ Increase clickthrough rate through providing key information&#x20;

➡️ Help your project stand out and catch the eye of people

## ❓How To Use

Projects can book trending with a few simple steps via the main bot [t.me/spydefi\_bot](https://t.me/spydefi_bot)

Simply head to the bot and type /start and select 'Book Trending' and follow the instructions there.&#x20;

{% hint style="success" %}
Projects who have the SpyDefi buybot added to their Telegram group can enjoy a 50% benefit to trending.
{% endhint %}
