# SpyDefi Bot Ads + Mass DM

## What are they?

A SpyDefi mass DM is a direct message from the official [t.me/spydefi\_bot](https://t.me/spydefi_bot) , to all 140,000 life time users of the bot. Projects can choose a banner, and accompanying text, to be sent.

Bot ads are adverts that appear on the bottom of multiple places in the SpyDefi ecosystem: i) some achievement posts in the SpyDefi feed (shared by KOLs) and ii) at the bottom of some user scans on the main bot. These are in the form of a banner (only appears on user scans) and a short 35 character description, hyperlinked to a destination of the project's choice.&#x20;
