# KOL Club

There are 3 features users can enjoy:

#### 📝 Report

KOLs can choose a time period (3 days, 1 week, 1 month, even a year), select the number of notable calls to display for that period (e.g top 5, top 10, top 15) and SpyDefi will generate a detailed channel report for the stated period for your channel&#x20;

#### 🕰 Recap

KOLs can choose a time period (e.g 3 days, 1 week, 1 month, even a year), select the number of notable calls to display for that period (e.g top 5, top 10, top 15) and SpyDefi will generate a recap of all the calls for the stated period for your channel.&#x20;

#### 📊 Project

KOLs can provide the contract address of a project they have called on your channel, and SpyDefi will generate a 'call flex card' (with up to 12 different randomly generated font possibilities) alongside data, for KOLs to share their Xs on their channel.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Access is selective and needs to be manually granted, if interested, and a SpyDefi listed KOL, reach out to [t.me/spydefisupportbot](https://t.me/spydefisupportbot) to enquire about eligibility .
{% endhint %}
