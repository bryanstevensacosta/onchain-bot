# Customized Filters&#x20;

It essentially provides a blank canvas for users to sculpt their feed to see exactly, what they want, when they want.

The tool is broken down into three sections:

#### 👤 KOL Settings

Adjust which KOLs calls appear in your feed, based on parameters such as consistency and average X or even utilize our manual inclusion/exclusion function.&#x20;

#### 📈 Project Settings

Adjust which projects appear in your feed based on parameters such as marketcap, chain and total KOL calls.&#x20;

#### 🌐 Word Scanning

The ability to be alerted on calls that either: include your selected key words in the KOL's post or in the project name that was called. A tool to assist in tapping into both KOL knowledge and current trends/metas.
