# KOLs Settings

In the KOL settings section, users can adjust which KOLs are eligible to appear on their feed through the following parameters:&#x20;

#### 👤KOLs

Simply filter by specific channel, or even by entire network, equally you can exclude KOLs here you wish not to see in your set filters.

#### ⚖️ Consistency

Here you can select a minimum consistency % in order for a KOL’s post to appear in your feed.

#### 📊 Average MC

Filter by the average marketcap of projects called by the KOL.

#### 📈 Average X

Filter by the average X the KOL is achieving on their calls.

#### 👑 First Caller

Toggle this on to only see alerts when the KOL is the first caller for the project in the SpyDefi network, historically this tends to raise the chances that the launches will be recent and at a lower marketcap.
