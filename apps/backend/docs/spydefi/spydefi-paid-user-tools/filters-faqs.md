# Filters FAQs

<details>

<summary>1) Is there a free trial?</summary>

No, we believe the docs alone give enough insight into the Filters tool and we do not offer free trials.&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>2) What is the best way to use filters effectively? </summary>

SpyDefi filters provides essentially a blank canvas, whereby users can tailor their own bespoke feed by adjusting a range of provided parameters. Therefore each filters users experience differs, relative to the effort they put in in terms of curating and monitoring their feed.

We don't provide specific advice, but many historically have found that utilizing parameters 'less' is 'more', and if adding an excessive amount of parameters, it lessens the pool of eligible KOLs/calls to near 0.&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>3) So if I set strict high settings, I will make money?</summary>

Not necessarily. SpyDefi Filters is a signals alert system based on calls made by external KOL via call channels. We do not control their actions, and can only provide historical data on their previous performance to help users make informed judgements. Higher performing KOLs themselves are still prone to poor judgement or being impacted by external factors themselves project side, as the saying goes, previous performance doesn't guarantee future results.&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>4) Can I have more than one set of filters running at once?</summary>

No, you can only set up one *set* of filters settings at one time, you can't have different sets of parameters running concurrently.

</details>

<details>

<summary>5) How do I subscribe?</summary>

Head to [t.me/spydefi\_bot](https://t.me/spydefi_bot) , type /start and select 'Subscribe To User Tools'

</details>
