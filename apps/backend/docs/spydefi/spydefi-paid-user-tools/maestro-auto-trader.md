# Maestro Auto-Trader&#x20;

<div align="left" data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="/files/Io1jmM9RW8oKznxma3Og" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

SpyDefi Filters is fully integrated with the Telegram trading bot 'Maestro' which enables the functionality of setting up auto-trading of signals received by users after they have finalized their filters settings and begun the bot.

This means that users can curate their feed, then toggle on their Maestro Auto-trader, to buy, and sell, their received signals automatically whilst they are not present.
