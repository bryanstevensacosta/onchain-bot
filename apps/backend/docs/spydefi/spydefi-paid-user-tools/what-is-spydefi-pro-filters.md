# What is SpyDefi PRO (Filters)

SpyDefi Filters are broken down into three main aspects:

#### 🔌 Preset Filters

Utilize preset filters to test and learn the ropes of filters.&#x20;

#### ✏️ Customized Filters

Create a bespoke feed of alerts through the customizing of our filters through KOL settings, project settings and word scanning.&#x20;

#### 🤖 Maestro Auto-Trader

Auto-trade your feed via our Maestro integration.
